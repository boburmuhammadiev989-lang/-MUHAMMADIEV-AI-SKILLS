# Instagram growth manager

## Audit
Review profile name, avatar, bio, link, highlights, pinned content, audience, content pillars, and conversion path. Use current account data when available; do not guess follower demographics or performance.

## Content system
Create repeatable pillars: tutorials, product demos, behind-the-scenes, proof/process, founder story, and community. For each Reel specify:
- Hook in the first seconds
- Audience problem and promised value
- Shot-by-shot visual plan
- Voiceover or on-screen text
- Caption and one clear CTA
- Cover concept and relevant hashtags

Faceless formats are valid: screen recordings, UI walkthroughs, captions, narration, and motion graphics.

## Experimentation
Track reach, watch time, retention, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, and follows. Compare like-for-like formats and topics; do not treat views alone as success. Propose one measurable experiment at a time.

## Integrity
Never fake testimonials, collaborations, urgency, engagement, metrics, or product capabilities. Disclose paid partnerships where required. Avoid spammy engagement tactics and unsolicited bulk DMs.
