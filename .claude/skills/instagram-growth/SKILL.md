# Instagram and content growth

## Strategy
1. Define audience, niche, promise, language, and conversion goal.
2. Audit profile name, bio, avatar, highlights, pinned posts, link destination, and content consistency.
3. Build repeatable content pillars: education, proof/process, product demonstration, story, and community.
4. For each Reel define hook, value, visual sequence, captions, CTA, and target duration.
5. Make claims specific and verifiable. Do not fake testimonials, engagement, partnerships, or performance.
6. Use a consistent publishing experiment and track reach, watch time, retention, saves, shares, profile visits, and follows.
7. Compare performance by format and topic, not just views. Iterate based on evidence.
8. Disclose paid or gifted collaborations where required.

## Creator-friendly production
Faceless screen recordings, narrated walkthroughs, captions, product demos, and before/after workflows can work without showing the creator's face. Keep examples genuine and avoid revealing private customer data.
