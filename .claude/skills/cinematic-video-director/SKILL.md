# Cinematic video director

## Pre-production
1. Confirm goal, audience, platform, aspect ratio, language, duration, CTA, and available generation tool.
2. Separate verified facts from fictional story elements. Never invent product features, UI, statistics, or outcomes.
3. Write and lock the exact dialogue before writing prompts. Include speaker, pronunciation, emotion, pacing, and lip-sync notes.
4. Create a continuity bible covering faces, age, hair, wardrobe, body proportions, voice, location, props, lighting, camera geography, and color grade.
5. Break the film into tool-compatible shots. Use 10-second clips as the default for this creator unless another duration is requested.

## Prompt each shot
Specify opening composition, subject action, camera/lens/movement, lighting, exact dialogue, facial emotion, motion graphics, sound effects, music, ending frame, and transition. Repeat all critical continuity details in every clip; never assume the model remembers previous generations.

## Constraints
No extra or omitted dialogue, gibberish text, warped hands/faces, continuity resets, random props, unapproved logos, or unsupported claims. Keep text overlays short and verify spelling in post.

## Delivery
Return numbered clip prompts, exact script, audio plan, edit order, transitions, captions, export ratio, and a final QA checklist. Prefer one test generation before spending credits on a full sequence. Disclose tool limits and watermarks honestly.
